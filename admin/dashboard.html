            Version1.1.0November 8, 2026

            Employee and device monitoring update

            Improves laptop assignment and adds secure device registration and connection monitoring.

            • Assign and reassign laptops to employees while retaining assignment history
            • Register and revoke devices with unique device IDs and authorized MAC address records
            • Show laptop registration, online/offline status, and last heartbeat in employee and laptop views
            • View and filter device connection history by employee, laptop, and date
            • Add a Windows monitoring agent that securely sends device heartbeats
            • Support employee-specific or all-employee website blocking schedules
            • Add login light and dark mode and simplify laptop registration fields
